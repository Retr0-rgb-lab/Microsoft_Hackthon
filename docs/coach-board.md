# Coach board v1

Product spec. The model, the data and the front end are implemented according to this document. Interactions, objects and entry points not written here are not part of v1.

Names aligned with the repository:

- Coordinates and files use v0.1 of `touchline/scripts/synth/FORMAT.md` (centre spot, metres, 105×68, y up, `attack_dir`).
- The class model uses `docs/v1-model-selection.md`: a four-class linear softmax, `pass` / `shoot` / `dribble` / `hold`. The weights file is `src/inside_the_game/system1/weights.json`.
- Route ranking uses `pitch_control_x_xt` from `inside_the_game.system1.completion`.
- 3D and 2D reuse the broadcast camera and the top-down camera from `touchline/scene`, and the coordinate mapping there: data `(x, y, z)` → three.js `Vector3(x, z, -y)`.
- The sample pitch is `touchline/data/samples/sample_25hz_10min/`, with `match_id` `SYN-SINGLE-0001`.

## 1. The product in one sentence

On a pausable three-dimensional pitch the coach personally draws passes, carries, shots and formations; while paused the model fills in one action class and one route in a pale tint, and only once the coach accepts does it become ink; a rollout is a passage of playback generated by the rule simulator from that formation.

Two uses, one board: after the match, explain this moment to the players; before the match, set the formation and then generate a rollout. The league browser is not an entry point to this product.

## 2. One session

A session has only two ways to open.

**After the match.** The coach opens a v0.1 directory (`metadata.json` + `tracking.jsonl`, optionally with `events.jsonl`). The default sample is the 25 Hz clip above. Playback uses that directory's `frame_hz`. Space pauses, space again resumes. On pause the playhead lands on the frame `frame_id = round(t_ms * frame_hz / 1000)`.

**Before the match.** The coach opens an empty board. The board holds 22 players, with positions taken from `BASE` in `touchline/scripts/synth/generate.py` converted to centre-circle coordinates through `out_xy`: HOME is placed as-is, AWAY is mirrored by `x → -x`. Role order matches `POSITIONS` in that file, `H01`–`H11` and `A01`–`A11`. Defaults are `attack_dir = +1` (HOME attacks +x), score `[0, 0]`, `t_ms = 0`. This board has only one frame and no match progression.

After either way of opening, the coach does the same set of things:

1. **Look.** The camera switches between "Broadcast" (3D perspective, broadcast) and "Top-down" (2D god view, top-down). The two cameras see the same players, the same ink, the same ghost. Switching does not change coordinates. Button labels follow the scene, "Broadcast" and "Top-down"; keys `1` / `b` are broadcast, `2` / `t` are top-down. Broadcast is the default.
2. **Pause.** The ghost and the four class probabilities appear only while paused. During playback only the players and the ink already drawn are shown.
3. **Draw.** Drawing works at any time, including when the model is wrong, including when there is no ghost. Whatever is drawn is ink, immediately a solid line.
4. **Accept.** While paused, `Tab` or a click on the ghost stroke collapses the ghost into ink. `Esc` puts away this frame's ghost until the frame changes. The ghost never turns into ink by itself.
5. **Edit formation.** Enter "Formation". The tracked players inside an after-the-match clip cannot be dragged; the formation is a separate layer of 22 slots. On the before-the-match board those 22 players are the formation and are dragged directly. Dragging changes the same `(x, y)` in both cameras.
6. **Rollout.** The coach sets a duration, 30 seconds by default, generates a new v0.1 directory from the current formation, and then switches this board over to play that directory. A rollout is playback of a generated clip. Ink does not drive the simulation.

To carry a seen formation into a rollout after the match: on a paused frame run "Fill formation from current frame", copying that frame's 22 players' `x, y, facing` into the slots; roles still come from `metadata.players[].position`. Then enter "Formation" to edit, and generate.

Ball carrier: after the match use that frame's `ball_owner_id`. Before the match, or in the formation layer, click a player to put the ball at his feet (`z = 0`, horizontal distance ≤ 0.6 m, consistent with the FORMAT possession constraint); click the same player again and the ball is taken back, with `ball_owner_id` empty.

## 3. v1 does this, later does that

### v1

- Four kinds of ink: pass, carry, shot, hold marker. The hold marker is written only when a `hold` is accepted. Formation editing: 22 positions and roles.
- Ghost: the four class probabilities on a paused frame, plus **one** pale route. `hold` has no route.
- Accept: `Tab` or a click writes the class and that one route as ink. Curvature and action technique are not written along with it.
- Camera: broadcast and top-down toggled against each other, with the same mapping as `touchline/scene`.
- Rollout: the improved `touchline/scripts/synth/generate.py` generates the clip, and play can start with a kick-off or a carry from the coach-edited formation. Watching is playing that clip.
- Board file `board.json` (see section 4). Ink is written in the board file and is not written back into `tracking.jsonl` / `events.jsonl`.
- The class model changes training labels this round: the generator picks actions by value, then it is refitted on this machine. The output is still a four-class distribution plus one route, and the largest probability is not called optimal. Until the new weights arrive the board must run with no network and no Azure calls on the checked-in `weights.json`.
- After selecting a piece of ink the coach can change its coach fields, `Delete` removes it, `Ctrl+Z` undoes the most recent add, accept or delete.

### Later

- More complete ball and player physics: bounce, air resistance, turning and braking, together with changing the ball carrier's 6.5 m/s and everyone else's 7.2 m/s speed caps and the braking distance in `generate.py`. v1 does not touch these numbers.
- Tackles, duels, fouls, cards, and new event types.
- Narration. The Azure AI Foundry, `gpt-4.1-mini` and `responses_narrator` in `docs/v1-model-selection.md` do not enter the coach board.
- Treating Azure as a training or runtime dependency. Everything that can be done on this machine is done on this machine.
- Deleting or replacing the Touchline league interface (the `touchline/` dashboard, `data/league/`), the Scheme A broadcast shell in `web/`, the `touchline/scene` samples, and `fixtures/statsbomb/`.
- Driving the rollout with ink (letting the simulator play according to the arrows that were drawn).
- Video or CV as model input.
- Exporting finished video, share links, player accounts, a clip library and a season browser. v1 opens one v0.1 directory and plays the one rollout that was just generated.
- softmax predicting the receiver, curvature, pass height, shot technique.
- Putting a public model into the board's runtime.

## 4. Objects and fields

Coordinates are always centre spot, metres. Angles are radians, `0` towards +x, counter-clockwise positive, range `(-π, π]`, same as `facing` in FORMAT. `team_id` is only `HOME` and `AWAY`.

### Frame

An after-the-match frame is one line of v0.1 `tracking.jsonl`. Fields the board reads: `frame_id`, `t_ms`, `period`, `attack_dir`, `in_play`, `phase`, `possession_team_id`, `ball_owner_id`, `score`, `players` (22 × `[player_id, x, y, vx, vy, facing]`), `ball` (`[x, y, z, vx, vy, vz]`).

Before the match, and in the formation layer, one frame is synthesised with the same fields: `frame_id = 0`, `t_ms = 0`, `period = 1`, `in_play` is `true` when a ball carrier has been designated and `false` otherwise, `phase` is `carry` when there is a ball carrier and `restart_setup` otherwise, all velocities are 0, `ball.z = 0`.

Reject a directory whose `coordinate_system.origin` is not `centre_spot`, or whose pitch is not 105×68. `touchline/generate.py` (the league generator with a top-left origin at 5 Hz) does not enter the board and does not enter the model.

### Board file `board.json`

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `schema` | fixed `"coach-board/1"` |
| `source` | `"clip"` or `"setup"` |
| `clip_dir` | directory of the after-the-match clip or of the rollout output; `null` before the match and before any rollout |
| `camera` | `"broadcast"` or `"top"` |
| `formation` | the formation object below |
| `ink` | array of ink |

The ghost is not written to the file. It is computed from the paused frame and the current weights.

### Formation

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `label` | a short name written by the coach, default `"4-3-3"`. Only a label, never parsed into positions |
| `attack_dir` | `+1` or `-1`, same meaning as FORMAT: whether HOME attacks +x |
| `slots` | length 22 |

Each slot: `player_id`, `team_id`, `role`, `x`, `y`, `facing`.

`role` allows only the 11 that already exist in `generate.py`: `GK`, `LB`, `LCB`, `RCB`, `RB`, `LCM`, `DM`, `RCM`, `LW`, `ST`, `RW`. Changing formation means changing coordinates, not inventing new role strings, and not typing `"3-5-2"` to place players automatically. `x` is within `[-52.5, 52.5]`, `y` within `[-34, 34]`. v1 neither adds nor removes players and has no substitutes.

### Pass ink

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ink_id` | unique within the board |
| `kind` | `"pass"` |
| `frame_id`, `t_ms` | the frame at which it was drawn or accepted |
| `team_id` | the passing player's team |
| `from_player_id`, `to_player_id` | player A to player B, both required |
| `start`, `end` | `[x, y]`, taken from the two players' positions on that frame when accepted |
| `path` | polyline. A straight line is `[start, end]`. A curve is the 9 points of a quadratic Bézier (start, 7 samples, end) |
| `style` | `"ground"` or `"loft"`. A coach field. Written as `"ground"` when a ghost is accepted |
| `curve` | `"straight"` or `"curved"`. Written as `"straight"` when a ghost is accepted |
| `control` | one control point `[x, y]` for a curve; `null` for a straight line |
| `source` | `"coach"` or `"ghost_commit"` |
| `visible_ms` | default `4000` |

How to draw: click A, then click B. Afterwards `style` can be changed, or `curve` set to `curved` and a control point dragged out. The model does not fill `style` or `curve`.

The correspondence between `style` and the generator's event fields is used only when ink is later handed back to the simulator: `ground` → `pass_height: ground`, `loft` → `pass_height: high`. v1 rollouts do not read ink.

### Carry ink

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `"carry"` |
| `from_player_id` | the ball carrier |
| `start`, `end` | `[x, y]`. `end` is the target point the coach clicks, or the endpoint of the ghost route |
| `path` | the straight polyline between start and end |
| `direction_rad` | derived from `start → end`, same convention as `facing` |
| `source`, `frame_id`, `t_ms`, `team_id`, `visible_ms` | same as pass |

No `style`, no `curve`. How to draw: click the ball carrier, then click the target point on the pitch. A softmax `dribble` is written as `carry` once accepted.

### Shot ink

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `"shot"` |
| `from_player_id` | the shooter |
| `start`, `end` | `[x, y]`. `end` is the ground aiming point |
| `path` | `[start, end]` |
| `direction_rad` | the arrow bearing, derived from `start → end` |
| `angle_rad` | the same geometry as `direction_rad`: changing the angle rotates `end` around the shooter with the length unchanged; dragging `end` writes the angle back |
| `style` | `"unset"`, `"driven"`, `"placed"`, `"chip"`. Written as `"unset"` when a ghost is accepted, then chosen by the coach |
| `source` and the time fields | same as pass |

How to draw: click the shooter, then click the aiming point. `style` does not change `path`. A softmax `shoot` is written as `shot` once accepted.

### Hold ink

`kind` is `"hold"`. Fields: `ink_id`, `frame_id`, `t_ms`, `team_id`, `player_id`, `at` (`[x, y]`, at the feet of that frame's ball carrier), `source`, `visible_ms`. No `path`. Produced only by accepting a `hold` ghost; the coach does not draw a separate kind of arrow from the toolbar.

### When ink is visible

Shown while the playhead is within `[t_ms, t_ms + visible_ms]`. When paused on its `frame_id` it stays shown. This is not the 8-second rule of the `touchline/scene` decision overlay; that rule belongs only to `decisions.json` in the scene sample, and the coach board does not read `decisions.json`.

### Ghost

Computed once while paused and while that frame's `ball_owner_id` is non-empty. Ball in the air, dead ball, no ball carrier: no ghost is shown. The goalkeeper is determined by `role == GK` on the slot or in the metadata, not hard-coded from a shirt number.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `frame_id`, `t_ms`, `player_id`, `team_id` | the ball carrier and his team |
| `probabilities` | `pass`, `shoot`, `dribble`, `hold`, summing to 1, in the same order as `labels` in `weights.json` |
| `suggested_class` | the class with the largest probability. On a tie, take whichever appears first in the order `pass`, `shoot`, `dribble`, `hold`, same as `topAction` in `touchline/scene/src/decisions.ts` |
| `route` | the single route below, or `null` |
| `score_method` | fixed `"pitch_control_x_xt"` when there is a route |
| `weights_version`, `fit_on` | copied from the current weights file, for the interface footnote |

`route`: `kind` (`pass` / `carry` / `shot`), `target_id`, `path`, `end`, `score`.

- `suggested_class == pass`: `kind = pass`, `target_id` is the receiver, `path` is a two-point segment.
- `dribble`: `kind = carry`, `target_id` is the ball carrier himself (same carry convention as `PitchPath`), `path` is a 5-point straight line (`CARRY_STEPS`).
- `shoot`: `kind = shot`, `target_id` is the shooter, `path` is two points.
- `hold`: `route = null`.

When that class has no legal route, `route = null` and only the probability and the class name are shown, with no arrow drawn.

Drawing: ghost opacity 0.35, ink opaque. The ghost has exactly one route. No second pass is drawn, and the scene overlay's scheme of "solid primary pass, semi-transparent secondary pass, dashed carry" is not used. The probability panel is visible at the same time: the four numbers, `suggested_class`, and the route's `score`. These three numbers are not multiplied into a single score.

Interface wording calls `suggested_class` "suggested". The words "optimal" and "best action" are forbidden. Beside the probability panel there is a fixed sentence: "The class comes from a softmax fitted on heuristic labels, not an optimal decision."

### Which frame the class and the route come from

The six features are the same as in `features_from_tracking`: `ball_x`, `ball_y`, `centrality`, `pressure`, `minute`, `goal_diff`. The actor is `ball_owner_id`, the team is his `team_id`, `minute` comes from `t_ms`, and the score comes from `score` on the frame. This row lives only in memory and is not written to `events.jsonl`.

Candidate routes, in the priority order below, take exactly one set:

1. If `events.jsonl` contains a `decision_point` whose `available_at_ms` equals that frame's `t_ms` and whose `player_id` equals the ball carrier: use `decide_from_tracking`. The pass candidates are the passes in that event with `offside: false`, sorted by completion score, and the ghost uses only the first one (`primary_pass`). Carry uses that event's carry endpoint; if there is none, the 6-metre fallback below. Shot uses the `end_location` of that event's shot option.
2. Otherwise compute from the frame, without random numbers:
   - **Pass:** the current `(x, y)` of the other players on the same team. The offside test uses the simplified Law 11 of FORMAT section 4 (opponent's half, closer to the opponent's goal than the ball, closer to the opponent's goal than the second-last opponent), and offside players are removed. The one with the highest completion score wins. On a tie the lexicographically smaller `target_id` wins, consistent with the ordering in `tracking.py`.
   - **Carry:** 6.0 metres along that team's attacking direction, 0 laterally (`FALLBACK_CARRY_M`), clamped inside the pitch. The random 3–12 metres with ±7 metres laterally from `heuristic_v0` is not used.
   - **Shot:** the centre of the goal being attacked, `(52.5 * d, 0)`, where `d` is the attacking flip from FORMAT section 6 (`d = +1` when that team attacks +x). When `role == GK` there is no shot candidate and no carry candidate, consistent with `choose_action` skipping the number 1.

`suggested_class` decides which one is drawn. The pass completion ranking does not change the softmax, and the softmax does not pick the receiver.

## 5. What "reasonable" means

A suggestion is one distribution plus one route ranked by completion score.

- **The distribution** answers "does this moment look more like a pass, a shot, a carry or a hold". It is a four-class softmax. The current `weights.json` is fitted on the 124 `decision_point`s of `SYN-SINGLE-0001`, one 10-minute match at 0-0. The labels come from `heuristic_v0`: about 6% random shots near the box, about 36% random carries, and the rest weighted random passes. The features are only the six scalars above. The `goal_diff` weight is 0 in that file. Calling the largest probability an optimal decision does not match this fit.
- **One route** answers "if the suggested class is taken, where does it land geometrically". A pass is one receiver, a carry is one direction, a shot is one aiming direction. The only ranking key is `pitch_control_x_xt` (control is a Gaussian influence share with σ = 7 m, xT is a static surface in the attacking coordinate frame, and both live in `completion.py`; neither is a transition matrix fitted on real matches). The completion score is not a win probability and is not multiplied by the probability.
- **Technique** is not part of the suggestion. Ground or lofted, straight or curved, and a shot's `driven` / `placed` / `chip` exist only in coach ink.

The coach may decline. When declined, nothing on the board changes. Accepting copies only the class and the route, with `style` and `curve` taking the defaults from section 4.

After refitting the output is still a distribution, and the largest probability is still not called optimal. Training rows come only from our own v0.1 synthetic `decision_point`s. The label becomes the option the generator selected by value, with the value defined by `value_v1` in `docs/coach-board-model.md`, no longer imitating the draw of roughly 36% carries and 6% shots in `heuristic_v0`. The class mapping can stay: pass → `pass`, shot → `shoot`, a carry with progression or with no opponent pressing → `dribble`, a short carry with an opponent within 3 metres → `hold`. StatsBomb, the league generator and paid APIs are not read into the weights.

## 6. Decisions added relative to the original list

These are already written into sections 3–5; only the reasons are given here.

- **Ink lands separately in `board.json`.** Accepting a ghost by editing `events.jsonl` would change the `heuristic_v0` labels, and the sample could no longer be treated as the same generation.
- **The ghost appears only while paused, only for the ball carrier, and only one.** The original wording was to complete it after pausing; the scene overlay's primary pass plus secondary pass plus carry is a different demonstration, and bringing it in would become three suggestions.
- **`hold` is written as a marker with no arrow.** One of the four classes is hold, so accepting it must have an object to write.
- **Frames without a `decision_point` also get a ghost.** The coach will pause on arbitrary frames and on the before-the-match board; binding the ghost to just those 124 events would leave the before-the-match board with no suggestion. Candidates use the deterministic rules of section 4, so that each client does not sample on its own.
- **A pass endpoint lands at that frame's player's feet, the carry fallback is a straight 6 metres, and the shot fallback is the goal centre.** The random endpoints and random shot depth in the generator are label noise and cannot serve as suggestions.
- **Offside passes do not enter that single route.** `decide_from_tracking` already drops `offside: true`.
- **The formation name is not parsed, and roles are the closed set of the existing 11.** Otherwise the front end would implement a formation grammar while the generator only understands coordinates and `POSITIONS`.
- **Post-match tracking is not draggable, the formation is a separate layer, and there is "fill formation from current frame".** Dragging players inside the clip directly would change historical frames; the two uses need one explicit copy.
- **Rollouts do not execute ink.** The original wording was to generate a clip that can start from the edited formation. Turning arrows into a script is a different strategy.
- **A rollout starts from a `carry` at his feet when a ball carrier has been designated, and otherwise still uses the existing kick-off, but with the 22 initial coordinates replaced by the formation.** This makes "play a spell from this formation" hold without writing new dead-ball rules.
- **v1 does not change the 6.5 / 7.2 speed caps.** They are tied to the braking distance in `generate.py`, and changing only the caps would distort ball-out and turning. Making actions look more like a ball is left for when braking and bounce are done together.
- **Probability, suggested class and completion score are not combined into one number.** Once combined, the interface would be left with a single "best action".
- **Delete and one-step undo.** Left unspecified, each client would build its own history.

If the data side evaluates a public pretrained model: it may be used to **produce training clips** only if it can emit tracking or `decision_point`s with v0.1 centre coordinates, 105×68 and `attack_dir`. It does not replace `generate.py`, does not enter the coach board process, and is not a completion condition for v1. If it does not match the format, it is not used.

## 7. Non-goals

- Calling the largest probability, or the route with the highest completion score, an optimal decision.
- Using the softmax to pick the receiver, or letting the model output curvature, pass height or shot technique.
- Playing football with a neural network inside a rollout. The rollout's executor is the rule simulation in `touchline/scripts/synth/generate.py`. Newly generated matches write `policy` as `value_v1`. The old seed 8 slice keeps `heuristic_v0` and is not taken for refitting.
- Putting the league coordinates of `touchline/generate.py` or `fixtures/statsbomb/` into the model or the board.
- Making Azure, Foundry narration or video understanding a dependency of the coach board.
- Deleting the league interface, the `web/` broadcast demo or the StatsBomb fixtures in this round.
- Rich physics, foul cards, replay according to ink, finished-video export, multiple users.

## 8. Implementation order once the four plans are aligned

The model, data and front-end notes are in `docs/coach-board-model.md`, `docs/coach-board-data.md` and `docs/coach-board-frontend.md` respectively. The items below cover where they disagree with each other.

- The value formula uses only `value_v1` from the model note: passes and carries are `success_prob × max(progress_m, 0)`, shots are `success_prob × 20`. No additional 12-metre deduction for losing the ball, and shots are not multiplied by 35.
- Train 20 matches, 20 minutes each, at 10 Hz, seeds 1001–1020. Hold out 2 matches, seeds 9001 and 9002; the training script rejects those two. While shots in training are fewer than 30, and fewer than 15 in the holdout, keep adding seeds. Do not overwrite `sample_25hz_10min`. Coach rollouts are still 25 Hz, 30 seconds by default, and can start from a kick-off from the formation.
- The formation is a separate layer. Players inside an after-the-match clip cannot be dragged. To produce a rollout, first run "fill formation from current frame". The anchors for off-ball running must be replaced by these 22 points, otherwise players walk back to the 4-3-3.
- Accepting `hold` writes a hold marker with no arrow. Pass style is only `ground` and `loft`, curvature only `straight` and `curved`. Shot style is `unset`, `driven`, `placed`, `chip`. The front-end note's `lofted`, `driven` passes and inside/outside curl are not adopted.
- Paused frames without a `decision_point` still get one deterministic ghost (product section 4). Training rows come only from `decision_point`s.

Implement in this order, and do not start the next step until the previous one has something visible:

1. Change the generator to `value_v1`, write the route fields, and let the formation serve as anchors. Play a few minutes of a sample locally and confirm that the selected option is the one with the highest value.
2. Change the features and the training script as described in the model note, and fit on this machine. If holdout log loss and route agreement do not pass, keep using the checked-in weights on the board and mark them uncalibrated.
3. Create `touchline/board/`, port 5175. First only play the clip, with broadcast and top-down switchable.
4. While paused, draw the four class probabilities and one pale route; `Tab` or a click collapses them into ink.
5. Hand drawing, the formation layer, generating a rollout from the formation and playing it. The generator does not run in the browser; the rollout is written beforehand by local Python.
