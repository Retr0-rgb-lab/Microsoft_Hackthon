Training target and limits

Location value is trained on passes, carries, shots and goals from real matches: the target is a goal within at most ten eligible on-ball actions of the same simplified possession. Pass and shot base rates come from real events. The lane adjustment, and the retention weights for carrying and holding, remain rule assumptions. Without real body orientation, preferred foot or first-touch quality, no capability score is offered.

The observed outcome never enters the pre-action score, and a failed pass is not automatically a wrong choice. An actual carry is matched by action type only: the alternative carry routes are not the player's original intent. This page does not call an LLM.
