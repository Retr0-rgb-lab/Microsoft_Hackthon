    Model assumptions & research
    This baseline uses probability-weighted outcome values minus current value, following the expected-usefulness formulation in un-xPass §2.1. Its feasibility and spatial-value functions are hand-defined and uncalibrated. They are not trained un-xPass, VAEP, xT, xG, or real goal probabilities. Player foot preference and body orientation are unknown. Only candidate ground passes are compared; the result does not claim passing is better than shooting or carrying.
